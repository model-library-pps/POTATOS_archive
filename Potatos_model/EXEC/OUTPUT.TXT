
The variables used in the 'n' DAY OUTPUT have the following meaning:

CROP DATA
DAY= day number (1= January 1); 
WTUB= weight of tubers (kg DM/ha);  
TAGB= weight of total above-ground biomass inclusive tubers (kg DM/ha); 
RDRY= reduction photosynthesis by water stress (-); 
SMACT= actual soil moisture content (cm3/cm3);
TRAIN= total precipitation (mm); 
TIRR=  total irrigation (mm);
TTRANS= total transpiration (mm); 
TESOIL= total soil evaporation (mm); 
TDRAIN= total water loss by drainage below maximum root zone (mm); 
TRUNOF= total water loss by runoff (mm);
WTOT= total amount of water in maximum root zone (mm); 
WAVT= total amount of water available in maximum root zone (mm); 
PARAB= total intercepted PAR (MJ/m2); 
PAR= total incoming PAR (MJ/m2)



The variables used in the SUMMARY OUTPUT and the YIELD STATISTICS have the
following meaning:

Year= year number; 
PL= date of planting; 
EMERG= date of emergence; 
TUBI= date of tuber initiation; 
HALT= date of harvest or maturity (leaves dead); 
WTU= weight of tubers (kg DM/ha); 
TAGB= total above-ground biomass inclusive tubers (kg DM/ha); 
HI= harvest index (kg tubers / kg above-ground biomass incl. tubers); 
PARAB= total intercepted PAR (MJ/m2);
RUE= radiation use efficiency (g DM (total above ground biomass) / MJ 
    intercepted PAR );
DWAV= change in water available in maximum root zone (mm);
RAIN= total precipitation (mm);
ESOIL= total soil evaporation (mm);
TRANS= total crop transpiration (mm);
DRAIN= total loss of water by drainage below maximum root zone (mm); 
RUNOF= total loss of water by surface runoff (mm);
IRR= total irrigation (mm);
WUE= water use efficiency (g DM total above ground / kg water lost by 
    evaporation and transpiration)



The variables used in the BATCH INPUT file have the
following meaning:

RUNNAME= name of run in output files (max. 5 characters);
IYEAR= first year of run;
INYEAR= number of years;
IDPL= date of planting;
IDEM= date of emergence;
IFINIT= maximum number of days for single run (d);
STATR= weather station name (max. 5 characters);
SENSP= multiplied with rainfall data for sensitivity analysis;
SENSV= multiplied with vapour pr. data for   ,,        ,,    ;
SENSW= multiplied with windspeed data  for   ,,        ,,    ;
SENSR= multiplied with radiation data  for   ,,        ,,    ;
SENST= added to temperature data       for   ,,        ,, 
             (inclusive vapour pressure correction); 
CO= atmospheric CO2 concentration (ppmv);
ISOIL= number of soil data file (1 --> SOILP1.DAT; 2 --> SOILP2.dat);
ICROP= number of crop data file (1 --> CROPP1.DAT; 2 --> CROPP2.DAT);
IOPT= indicates type of weather data file (1--> CALL WEATHR (i.e. 
       AB weather data format, e.g. in file WAGE80.DAT), 
       not 1 --> CALL DBMRD (binary data sets)); 
IRRI= 0 (no irrigation) or 1 (irrigation);
IOUT= time interval for output (d);
WTRDIR= directory where weather data are stored;
REMARK= remark, printed on top of output file;
CONTIN= run for new site or years (Y or N)
